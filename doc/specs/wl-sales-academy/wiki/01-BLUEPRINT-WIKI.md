# Blueprint Wiki

## Purpose

The Blueprint of the Sale is the core training structure. The platform trains reps to execute the process consistently, clearly, and professionally.

## Canonical Top-Level Steps

1. Meet & Greet
2. Agenda
3. Breakfast / F.O.R.M.
4. Discovery / Survey
5. Break & Remake the Pact
6. Property Tour
7. Model Suite
8. Screen Tour & Flower
9. Point of Confirmation
10. Programs
11. T.O. Pricing

## Sub-Lessons

These are important but should not be top-level Blueprint steps unless explicitly approved:

- First Visit Incentives.
- 3-Way Pitch.
- Home Away from Home Program.
- Fee Disclosure.
- Hotel comparison.
- Timeshare comparison.
- Vacation club highlights.

## Step States

- Completed: green.
- Current: gold.
- Locked/upcoming: gray.

## Step Detail Pattern

Each step should include:

- Purpose.
- Required behaviors.
- Video/watch card.
- Script/talk track.
- Audio/delivery practice.
- Checklist.
- Common mistakes.
- Roleplay CTA.
- Manager evaluation criteria.
- Compliance note when applicable.

## Compliance Reminder

Training should reinforce clarity, professionalism, and client respect. Fee and pricing training must make important conditions clear.
