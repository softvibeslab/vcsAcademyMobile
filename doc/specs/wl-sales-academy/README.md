## Purpose

This folder is the working knowledge base for building **WL | White Label Sales Academy**, a premium AI-powered sales training and performance platform for resort and vacation club sales teams.

The goal is to give Cursor and developers enough context to build the app from the approved mobile-first black-and-gold designs while preserving the official Blueprint of the Sale, role-based access, compliance requirements, Smart Agent behavior, GoalSheet logic, roleplay flows, and test expectations.

## Non-Negotiables

- This is not a generic LMS.
- The official Blueprint order must remain intact.
- Sensitive pricing, fees, incentives, internal worksheets, and rep performance data must be protected.
- Fee and pricing training must reinforce clear disclosure.
- Smart Agent must coach ethically and professionally.
- Server-side permission checks are required for sensitive actions.
- Designs should follow the premium mobile-first black-and-gold WL Sales Academy direction.
